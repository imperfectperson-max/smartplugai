## 🎯 Strategy Overview

```
COMPETITION STRATEGY: 3 PHASES

  Year 1 → Academic recognition + credibility building
  Year 2 → Technology prizes + R400k+ in funding
  Year 3 → Seed funding catalysts + R2M+ in awards

TOTAL PRIZE POTENTIAL (3 years): R2M+
```

Competition wins serve three purposes:
1. **Validation** — independent judges confirm the technology works
2. **Funding** — prize money funds each development phase
3. **Network** — judges, sponsors, and co-competitors become investors and partners

## 🎓 Year 1: Academic Recognition (Months 1–12)

**Goal**: Establish credibility, validate SA-NILM research, win initial funding.

| Competition | Category | Prize | Timing | Key Differentiator |
|------------|---------|-------|--------|-------------------|
| University Research Day | Research | R10,000 | Month 8 | First SA NILM dataset |
| SA Young Researchers' Symposium | ML/AI | R20,000 | Month 10 | 91% accuracy on SA data |
| IEEE Student Paper Contest | Engineering | $1,000 (~R19k) | Month 12 | Novel SA-specific approach |

**Total Year 1 potential**: ~R49,000

### Year 1 Pitch Focus

```
YEAR 1 NARRATIVE:
  "We solved a problem nobody else tackled:
   South Africa's appliances don't behave like UK or US appliances.
   We built the first SA-specific dataset and achieved 91% accuracy —
   47% better than the baseline."
```

**Demo for Year 1**:
- Live laptop demo of ML model classifying SA appliances
- Charts showing SA accuracy vs. foreign-trained models
- Load shedding event visualisation

## 📋 Preparation Checklist

### 6 Months Before Competition

- [ ] Research competition judging criteria and past winners
- [ ] Align our strongest assets to judging criteria
- [ ] Begin building the required demo components
- [ ] Register interest or preliminary application

### 3 Months Before

- [ ] Draft pitch deck tailored to competition audience
- [ ] Prepare demo hardware and backup hardware
- [ ] Record 2-minute demo video as backup
- [ ] Get external feedback from mentors

### 1 Month Before

- [ ] Finalise pitch to exactly fit time limit (usually 5–7 minutes)
- [ ] Practice with timer (target: 3 practice runs)
- [ ] Prepare Q&A answers for likely questions
- [ ] Test all demo hardware twice

### Competition Day

- [ ] Arrive 30 minutes early
- [ ] Test all hardware on-site before presenting
- [ ] Have backup demo video on USB
- [ ] Bring 5 printed one-pagers for judges
