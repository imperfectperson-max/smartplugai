## 🏆 Competitive Advantages

| Advantage | Description | Defensibility |
|-----------|-------------|--------------|
| SA-trained dataset | Only model trained on SA households, geysers, load shedding | High — 3 months of local data collection |
| Bank-grade security | ATECC608A secure element — industry-first for consumer IoT plugs in SA | High — hardware component + firmware expertise |
| Price | R500 vs $299–$699 for competitors | Medium — manufacturing scale required |
| Load shedding intelligence | Built-in load shedding detection and impact tracking | High — SA-specific feature |
| On-device ML | Inference runs on ESP32-S3 — no cloud dependency | Medium — TFLite Micro expertise |
| Open dataset | SA-NILM Dataset published — builds trust and citations | High — first-mover academic credibility |

## ⚠️ Risk Analysis

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|-----------|
| Hardware failure in prototype | Medium | High | Multiple prototypes, thorough testing, PCB v2 |
| ML accuracy drops on new appliances | Low | Medium | Continuous retraining, ensemble methods, federated learning roadmap |
| Security breach | Low | Critical | ATECC608A secure element, regular audits, bug bounty (R5k–R50k) |
| Competitor enters SA market | Medium | Medium | IP protection, first-mover advantage, SA dataset moat |
| Regulatory / SABS certification delay | Medium | High | Early planning, expert advisors, parallel certification tracks |
| Team turnover | Low | Medium | Cross-training, comprehensive documentation, competitive equity |
| Funding shortfall | Medium | High | Bootstrap approach, competition prize money, phased development |
| Load shedding ends (demand risk) | Low | Medium | Platform value extends beyond load shedding to energy optimisation |
